## Creating a Brain in 4 Steps

1. Navigate to **Brains** in the top navigation bar.
2. Click **+ Create Brain** in the upper right.
3. Configure the Core Parameters:
   * **Brain Name**: Give your brain a clear title (e.g., *Biotech Patent Reviewer*).
   * **Category & Folder**: Select an existing folder or create a new one.
   * **System Persona Instructions**: Define the exact role, tone, and reasoning guidelines.
4. Attach Reference Notes: Paste text or upload reference guidelines into the brain storage.
5. Click **Save Brain**.

Your newly created Brain is instantly available in the **Right Tools Panel** across AXON Agent, Talk, CoWorX, and Symposium.
