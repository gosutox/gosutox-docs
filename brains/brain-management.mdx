## Brain Organization & Governance

* **Folder Hierarchy**: Group domain brains by department, client account, or project.
* **Version Duplication**: Clone existing brains to iterate on persona prompts without overwriting production templates.
* **Cross-Application Attachment**: A single Brain can be attached to:
  * **Agent**: For written report synthesis.
  * **Talk**: For voice conversational embodiment.
  * **CoWorX**: For  as a team agent.
  * **Symposium**: For participating in multi-agent debates.
