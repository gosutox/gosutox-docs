## Real-World Conversational Personas

### 1. Executive Mock Interviewer
Attach an *Executive Recruiter Brain* to conduct high-pressure behavioral and technical interviews with realistic real-time follow-ups.

### 2. Socratic Academic Tutor
Attach a *Physics or Language Tutor Brain* for interactive, child-friendly Socratic learning that guides learners through questions rather than reciting answers.

### 3. Hands-Free Strategic Advisor
Discuss complex market dynamics while driving or walking, receiving concise spoken briefings grounded in real-time data.
