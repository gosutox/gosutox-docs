## Hands-Free Productivity

* **In-Car Executive Briefings**: Listen to oral summaries of newly filed SEC reports or morning market news.
* **Language Speaking Practice**: Engage in natural, flowing spoken conversations to hone pronunciation and vocabulary.
* **Spoken Brainstorming**: Voice your raw ideas aloud and let the agent organize them into structured notes.
