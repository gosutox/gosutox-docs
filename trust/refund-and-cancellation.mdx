## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

* **14-Day Refund**: Full 100% refund on unutilized token balances within 14 days of purchase.
* **Self-Serve Cancellation**: Cancel recurring subscriptions at any time with a single click in Account Settings.
* **Quota Retention**: Retain full access to remaining token balances through the end of the active billing cycle.
