## Absolute Zero-Training Commitment

AXON operates under an absolute legal guarantee: **Your proprietary data, attached files, uploaded knowledge bases, and conversational transcripts are NEVER used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model.**

### Security & Privacy Controls
* **Legal Invariant**: Enterprise agreements explicitly prohibit data ingestion for public model weights.
* **Encryption in Transit**: TLS 1.3 encryption across all client-server communication channels.
* **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256 encryption across all dedicated regional databases and secure cloud storage vaults.
* **Immediate Ephemeral Scrubbing**: Staged files in temporary memory buffers are automatically purged upon session completion.
