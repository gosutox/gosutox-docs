## How the Autonomous Debate Works

1. **Opening Statements**: Each expert persona delivers an initial strategic assessment grounded in live data and domain rules.
2. **Cross-Examination & Rebuttal**: Agents challenge each other's assumptions (e.g., the CFO challenges the CTO's compute cost estimates).
3. **Evidence Grounding**: Panelists pull live market feeds (SEC EDGAR, DART, Google Search) to verify controversial claims.
4. **Resolution & Betting**: Agents converge on the optimal path forward with explicit confidence scoring.
