## Assembling Your Panel

When launching a Symposium session, you can:
1. **Auto-Generate Expert Personas**: Simply input your topic (e.g., *"Evaluating an acquisition of an AI semiconductor startup"*), and Symposium will automatically generate 4 complementary domain expert personas.
2. **Attach Existing Custom Brains**: Manually select your own proprietary company Brains to represent your organization's legal, financial, and product guidelines.
