## Strategic Session Governance

AXON provides enterprise-grade session lifecycle management in the **Left Sidebar**.

### 1. Custom Category Folders
Create dedicated folders to group related workflows:
* *📁 M&A Due Diligence 2026*
* *📁 Quarterly Earnings & SEC Reports*
* *📁 Product Architecture & Patent Inventions*
* *📁 Legal Compliance & Contracts*

### 2. Drag & Drop Organization
Move active conversation sessions into folders with a single click, or rename sessions to reflect strategic milestones.

### 3. High-Speed Session Search
Quickly find historical discussions by keyword, date, or attached Brain ID.

### 4. Background Session Persistence
AXON automatically persists session state and active background agent tasks across device reboots and browser refreshes.
