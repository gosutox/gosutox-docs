## Dual Reasoning Modes

AXON Agent allows users to toggle between two operational reasoning modes in the bottom input bar:

### 1. Flash Mode (Default)
* **Latency**: Sub-second instantaneous stream.
* **Best For**: Direct factual questions, quick document summaries, live market lookups, and draft editing.
* **Token Efficiency**: Ultra-lean token consumption with direct tool-to-output delivery.

### 2. Think Mode (Deep Dynamic Thinking)
* **Latency**: 3 to 10 seconds of deep autonomous reflection.
* **Best For**: Complex multi-variable trade-off analysis, legal contract risk audits, multi-year financial forecasting, and code architecture design.
* **Live Thinking Blocks**: Watch the agent dynamically evaluate hypotheses, reject faulty assumptions, and cross-verify facts before emitting the final answer.
