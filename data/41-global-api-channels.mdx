## Verified Global Data Directory

AXON integrates over 41 verified global data channels:

### 1. Corporate & Regulatory Feeds
* **US SEC EDGAR**: Real-time 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K filings with XBRL extraction.
* **Korea DART (FSS)**: Real-time Korean corporate disclosures and audit reports.

### 2. Financial Markets & Banking
* **Global FX & Treasury Yields**: Live currency pairs, US Treasuries, and sovereign bond rates.
* **FDIC Bank Soundness**: Comprehensive bank health metrics, asset tiers, and risk indicators.
* **Macroeconomics**: Central bank interest rates, CPI inflation, and GDP data.

### 3. Copy-Paste Prompt Catalog
In the **API Info Drawer**, click the copy icon on any verified API to grab pre-formatted prompt templates:
```
"Query SEC EDGAR for NVIDIA's latest 10-Q filing. Extract gross margin and AI infrastructure revenue growth."
```
