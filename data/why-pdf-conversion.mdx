## The Vector Loss-Free Grounding Principle

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory PDF Pre-Processing Policy**:
> Word (), PowerPoint (), and Hangul () files must be converted (baked) to **PDF ()** prior to uploading.

### Why Are Raw Office Files Restricted?
1. **Parser Layout Skew**: Proprietary office XML formats frequently experience dropped tables, misplaced footnotes, and font substitution during server-side text extraction.
2. **Hallucination Vectors**: Missing table cells in a financial report cause AI models to hallucinate numbers.
3. **Exact Visual OCR Grounding**: Exporting to PDF preserves the **fixed vector layout, exact coordinates, and typography**. AXON's multimodal vision engine indexes the exact visual page, guaranteeing **100% loss-free grounding and citation accuracy**.
