## Welcome to AXON

Getting started with AXON requires zero complex configuration or prompt engineering. Follow these three simple steps to run your first mission-critical task.

### Step 1: Sign In & Claim 300,000 Free Tokens
1. Navigate to **[https://axon.gosutox.com](https://axon.gosutox.com)**.
2. Sign in with Google or create an account with your work email.
3. Select your legal data residency region:
   * **South Korea (Seoul DB)**: Strictly compliant with PIPA.
   * **United States & Global (Iowa DB)**: Strictly compliant with CCPA and GDPR.
4. Your account is immediately credited with **300,000 free tokens** upon registration.

### Step 2: Open AXON Agent & Choose a Mode
In the central launcher, click **AXON Agent**:
* **Flash Mode (Default)**: Instant sub-second responses for quick summaries and inquiries.
* **Think Mode**: Activates dynamic multi-step reasoning, where AXON reflects deeply on complex trade-offs before delivering answers.

### Step 3: Combine Live Data & Document Brains
Open the **Right Tools Panel** (or click the wrench icon):
* **Select a Brain**: Attach a specialized knowledge brain (e.g., *Legal Review Brain*).
* **Toggle Live APIs**: Turn on *SEC EDGAR* or *Korea DART*.
* **Attach Files**: Select up to 10 files from your Data Files vault.

```markdown
Example Prompt:
"Analyze Tesla's 2026 Q2 R&D expenditure from SEC EDGAR filings and compare it against our internal product roadmap document. Generate an executive summary table in HTML."
```

Within seconds, AXON cross-references live SEC filings with your uploaded documents, generating an interactive report with full citation links.
