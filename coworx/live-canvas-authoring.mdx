## Collaborative Live Canvas

* **Split-View Workspace**: Chat thread on the left, live interactive document canvas on the right.
* **Agent-Assisted Drafting**: Instruct summoned Brain agents to write sections, format tables, or audit legal clauses directly on the canvas.
* **One-Click Export**: Export the finalized collaborative canvas to Document Studio for PDF printing.
