## Channel Governance

* **Public vs. Private Channels**: Restrict sensitive discussions (e.g., executive compensation) to authorized team members.
* **Role-Based Access (ABAC)**: Control who can summon Brains, edit canvas documents, or invite external guests.
* **Audit Trail**: Every AI contribution and document edit is immutably logged for enterprise compliance.
