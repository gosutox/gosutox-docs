## Two-Pass PDF Printing Engine

Standard browser printing often cuts charts in half or omits offscreen widgets. AXON's proprietary **Two-Pass Rendering Sweep** guarantees:
1. **Pass 1 (Pre-Warm Sweep)**: Renders all offscreen charts, tables, and SVG elements into painted memory.
2. **Pass 2 (Print Sweep)**: Enforces clean page breaks, corporate header/footer metadata, and timestamp watermarks for boardroom-grade PDF output.
