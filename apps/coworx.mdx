## Collaborative Canvas

CoWorX offers an interactive workspace where humans and AI co-create reports, architecture diagrams, and strategic plans on a shared canvas.

### Features
* **Real-Time Synchronized State**: SWR-derived reactive data pipelines.
* **Multi-Format Export**: One-click export to Markdown, PDF, and interactive slides.
